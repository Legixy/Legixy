12

Trust Signal Psychology

01
Spacing Discipline = Control
Consistent 8px grid. Users perceive random spacing as carelessness. When every gap is intentional, the product feels in control of its environment.
02
Color Restraint = Seriousness
Max 2 accent colors on any screen. Red = danger only. Purple = AI only. Teal = success only. The restraint itself communicates that warnings mean something.
03
Serif for Authority
Instrument Serif on display headings. Law firms use serifs on letterheads for the same reason — tradition, precision, seriousness. The deliberate departure every competitor won't make.
04
Radius Hierarchy = Precision
Small radius (6px) on inputs, larger (12px) on cards. "We're careful where it counts, approachable where it helps." Uniform bubbly corners = consumer app, not legal software.
05
Space Around Danger
High-risk cards get extra vertical padding. Space signals "read this carefully." Same technique print designers use for pull quotes and critical callouts.
06
Flat CTAs = Serious Action
No gradient on primary buttons. Flat indigo says "consequential action." Gradients say "consumer app." Users making legal decisions need UI that takes those decisions seriously.
